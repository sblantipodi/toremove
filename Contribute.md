You can contribute to Luciferin by:

- Submitting pull requests for features, fixes, or [translations](https://github.com/sblantipodi/firefly_luciferin/wiki/Translations).
- Testing stable/development builds with your PC, HDMI, or Raspberry setup and reporting reproducible issues.
- Improving documentation, adding screenshots, or sharing tested controller and capture device setups.
- Registering on GitHub and giving stars to the project. Stars are more or less similar to a "like".
- Making a donation [![PayPal](https://img.shields.io/badge/donate-PayPal-blue)](https://www.paypal.com/donate?hosted_button_id=ZEJM8ZLQW5E4A)
