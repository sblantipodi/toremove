Aspect ratio settings keep the LEDs focused on the picture instead of black bars. They apply to both **PC screen capture and HDMI capture**, such as a widescreen movie on a TV.

You can easily adapt a **16:9** video on a **21:9** monitor by using the dedicated aspect ratio feature.

### Auto Aspect Ratio Detection

**Auto** detects black bars and switches between the supported layouts. Detection is debounced so a brief dark frame does not immediately change the capture area. If a source's persistent borders are not detected reliably, select the appropriate layout manually.
